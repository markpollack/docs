Construct a ready `Jury` before calling `vote()`.
Keep each member's actual requirement and evidence, and retain complete originals and attempted children.
A heterogeneous ready panel may combine different inputs without inventing a shared parent requirement.

| Composition | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| `VotingJury` / `SimpleJury` | Reduction over configured Judge opinions |
| Explicit `AllOf` through `Assignments` | Complete declared constituent requirement coverage |
| `EarsJury` / `Rfc2119Jury` | Complete selected native roster coverage |
| `Juries.meta(strategy, new NamedJury(...), ...)` | Reduction over complete member Jury results |
| `CascadedJury` | Configured routing across tiers, with attempted children retained |

## Opinion rules

Voting uses typed `Ballot` values. A Seat binds position, identity and optional declared weight; filtering cannot detach weight from its opinion.
`AllEligiblePassStrategy` retains the `allMustPass` wire token and applies to eligible opinions.
It is distinct from AllOf or native roster coverage; an unsettled required roster item cannot be dropped to obtain PASS.
Rule configuration is captured before execution and remains with failed/refused reductions when capture succeeded.
Explicit trusted factories reconstruct custom pure rules from complete retained configuration.

## Identity and refusal

One declared usable Judge/member preserves its whole identity rather than inventing an average or new support signal.
The original and its treatment remain separate when admission refuses an association, undeclared N/A, or unusable return.
A child refusal stays visible; cancellation and preservation limits propagate rather than disappearing into generic containment.

## Routing and policy

Routing controls which tier executes and keeps every entered attempt.
Opinion-only routing is unsuitable for native roster/AllOf descriptions with `KNOWN_NONE` opinions.
A refused final tier terminates INCONCLUSIVE with its original retained.
Application Policy runs separately against the final whole Verdict. It decides reliance and never rewrites routing or conclusion.
